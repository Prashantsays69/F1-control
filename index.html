PRIVACY POLICY & DATA PRACTICES

1. Zero Personal Data Collection: F1 // CONTROL operates entirely client-side. We do not track users, log IP addresses, store cookies for advertising, or collect personally identifiable information (PII).

2. External Telemetry Sources: Telemetry, timing, standings, and calendar records are requested directly by your browser from public, documented motorsport databases (Jolpica F1 API and OpenF1 API). No queries pass through proprietary intermediary servers.

3. Local Storage: Preferences such as active FIA season, timezone display, and cached API responses are stored strictly in your browser’s local storage (`localStorage`) to minimize redundant network bandwidth. You may clear this cache at any time in Settings.

4. Attribution: Designed and built by Prashant Ratnala as a professional motorsport data product.
